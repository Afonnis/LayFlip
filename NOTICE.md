# Third-party material

LayFlip's letter-pattern models (what tells it that "ghbdtn" is Russian "привет" and "hello" is
English) are counted from sentence collections published by other projects. The counts are built
into the program; the sentences themselves are not included.

- **[Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org)** sentences, licensed under
  [CC BY 2.0 FR](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/) - used for every language.
- **Wikipedia sentences** collected by the
  [Leipzig Corpora Collection](https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/en/download), licensed under
  [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) - used, together with Tatoeba,
  for Kazakh, Kyrgyz and Mongolian.
- **Keyboard layout tables** were read from the layouts that ship with Windows on the author's own
  machine.

Thank you to everyone who contributed sentences to these projects.
